## Navigating AI & Academic Paradox

## Overview

As Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT become increasingly common in education, this project explores the relationship between AI usage, academic performance, academic integrity, and workplace preparedness among university students.

This **primary quantitative research study** was based on survey responses collected from university students. The objective was to understand how students use AI, identify patterns in their responses, and examine whether AI usage is associated with academic performance and other aspects of student development.

## Research Methodology & Data Collection

The project was developed using primary survey data rather than a pre-existing dataset.

### Survey Design

A structured questionnaire was used to collect information about students' AI usage, academic experiences, and perceptions regarding AI in education. The collected responses were organized into quantitative and categorical variables for statistical analysis.

Key areas examined included:

* AI usage and adoption
* Academic performance
* Academic integrity
* Students' perceptions of AI
* Workplace preparedness
* Patterns in student responses

## Data Preparation & Statistical Analysis

The collected survey data was cleaned, organized, and prepared for statistical analysis. Multiple statistical techniques were applied to understand the distribution of responses and investigate relationships between variables.

### Descriptive Statistics

Measures such as **mean, median, standard deviation, and other dispersion measures** were used to summarize the overall characteristics of the collected responses.

### Z-Score Analysis

**Z-scores** were calculated to identify unusually high or low observations and examine potential outliers within the dataset.

### Correlation Analysis

Correlation analysis was used to examine the strength and direction of relationships between selected variables, particularly those related to AI usage and academic outcomes.

### Simple Linear Regression

A **Simple Linear Regression** model was used to investigate the relationship between AI usage and academic performance. This provided a statistical way to examine whether changes in one variable were associated with changes in the other.

### Data Visualization

Statistical results were presented using **box plots and other visualizations** to make patterns, distributions, and unusual observations easier to interpret.

## Key Findings & Insights

The analysis identified meaningful patterns in how university students interact with AI tools and how AI usage relates to academic outcomes.

The regression analysis indicated a **positive relationship between AI usage frequency and self-reported academic performance** within the surveyed sample. However, the findings also highlighted an important academic and ethical question: higher reliance on AI may improve short-term academic outcomes while potentially creating concerns around independent learning, academic integrity, and preparation for future workplace responsibilities.

The project therefore presents the **AI & Academia Paradox**: AI can serve as a useful productivity and learning tool, while excessive dependence may raise questions about students' development of independent problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.

## Key Deliverables

* Structured primary survey dataset containing student responses.
* Cleaned and organized data prepared for statistical analysis.
* Descriptive statistical analysis of student responses.
* Z-score analysis for identifying unusual observations.
* Correlation analysis of relevant variables.
* Simple Linear Regression examining AI usage and academic performance.
* Box plots and statistical visualizations for interpreting the data.
* Comprehensive research report discussing the academic, ethical, and workplace implications of AI adoption.
